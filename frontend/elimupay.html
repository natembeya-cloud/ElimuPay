Now onboarding schools in Bungoma County

School fees, collected and reconciled automatically.

ElimuPay is a simple, mobile-first way for small and medium-sized private schools in Kenya to manage fees — without black books, spreadsheets, M-Pesa messages or constant phone calls to parents. It connects to the school's existing M-Pesa Till or Paybill and automatically records and reconciles every payment against the right student, whether a parent pays KSh 200 or KSh 5,000.

How it works

Four steps, no black book

1

Parent pays

Through the school's existing M-Pesa Till or Paybill — nothing new to set up.

2

ElimuPay reconciles

The payment is matched to the correct student automatically.

3

Balance updates

The student's paid and outstanding amounts recalculate instantly.

4

Everyone is informed

The parent gets an instant confirmation; the school's dashboard updates.

Two simple roles

Everyone sees only what they need

ElimuPay isn't one crowded app — it's a private dashboard for the school and a simple pay-and-check page for parents.

School administrators

A private dashboard, once onboarded

  • Upload student records and their parents' contact details
  • Verify payments received on your designated Paybill or Till
  • See balances, collections and outstanding fees in real time
  • Send fee reminders and generate statements and reports
Parents

No app, no account, no cost

  • Look up a balance with a student's admission number
  • Pay any amount, any time, through the same M-Pesa channel
  • Get an instant confirmation after every payment
  • Never asked to pay anything extra to use ElimuPay
For school administrators

Your school's dashboard, activated after onboarding

Once your school agrees to come on board, we set up your account against your designated M-Pesa Paybill or Till and hand your administrator a private sign-in. From there, the dashboard is yours: upload your students and their parents, and verify incoming payments as they arrive.

Not onboarded yet?

Tell us about your school and we'll be in touch to arrange onboarding and activate your dashboard.

For parents

Pay fees and check a balance

Find your school, then find your child by name, grade or admission number — whichever you have to hand. No app, no account, no charge to you.

Why schools choose ElimuPay

Faster, simpler, more transparent fee collection

ElimuPay's goal isn't to become another M-Pesa provider or to hold school funds — it's to remove the manual work around payments you already receive.

Your own payment channel

Money still goes straight to your school's own M-Pesa Paybill or Till, exactly as it does today.

Automatic reconciliation

Every payment is matched to a student and reflected in their balance without manual entry.

Parent notifications

Instant confirmations after every payment, and balance reminders on a schedule the school controls.

Reports on demand

Professional fee statements and collection reports, ready without going through paper records.

Accessibility

Built to be usable by everyone

Administrators and parents use ElimuPay on a range of devices and abilities. These controls and choices are built into the site, not added after the fact.

  • Skip-to-content link for keyboard and screen reader users
  • Adjustable text size, saved for your next visit
  • High-contrast mode for low-vision users
  • Full keyboard navigation, including the dashboard tabs
  • Visible focus outlines on every interactive element
  • Labels on every form field, not placeholder text alone
  • Status messages announced to screen readers
  • Reduced motion respected for those who prefer it
